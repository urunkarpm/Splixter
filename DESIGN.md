## Brand & Style: Material 3 Expressive

Splixter utilizes the **Material 3 Expressive** design system. It emphasizes clarity, tonal depth, high-contrast typography, and tactile responsiveness while completely eliminating blurry glassmorphism or floating liquid blobs.

The aesthetic leverages structured whitespace, tonal containers, vibrant accent roles, and bold geometric shapes to make bill and group expense management intuitive, fast, and delightful.

## Tonal Layering & Elevation

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **Material 3 Expressive Tonal Surfaces**:
- **Background**: Soft neutral canvas (`#F7F5FA` Light, `#0F1117` Dark).
- **Cards / Containers**: Solid surface container layers (`#FFFFFF` Light, `#181B24` Dark) with clean 1px tonal borders (`#E0DCE8` Light, `#333846` Dark).
- **Active / Focused Elements**: Vibrant primary outline and elevation states.

## Shapes & Radii

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: 20dp - 24dp rounded corners for a modern, friendly container feel.
- **Interactive Buttons**: 16dp - 20dp radius with responsive bounce feedback.
- **Pills & Identity Badges**: Full pill shape (CircleShape / 100dp) for participant avatars and status tags.

## Color Roles & Expressive Accents

- **Primary**: Electric Indigo (`#493EE5` Light, `#9B8CFF` Dark) for primary calls to action.
- **Secondary & Success**: Emerald Mint (`#00B894`) for positive settlements and payer designations.
- **Tertiary & Highlights**: Sky Cyan (`#0284C7`) and Coral Rose (`#FF7675`) for distinct category indicators and alerts.
